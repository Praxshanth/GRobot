# About GRobot

GRobot is a self-hosted, multi-tenant AI operating layer for businesses. It gives a company
a team of AI agents that read the inbox, answer customers, qualify leads, run workflows and
act in the tools the business already uses, with a human approving anything that matters.

Our first vertical is **construction and home services**, where missed calls, slow quotes
and scattered follow-up cost real money.

## What we believe

- **Agents should act, not just chat.** Useful work means taking actions in real systems.
- **Humans stay in control.** Risky actions wait for approval; everything is audited.
- **Your data stays yours.** Self-hosted, tenant-isolated, secrets in a vault.
- **Open standards.** Integrations speak MCP and a vetted catalogue of Pieces, run in an
  isolated host with controlled egress.
